# Luminous Structure

Luminous Structure treats darkness as architectural space rather than emptiness. A flat field of near-black (`--bg` `#1e1e1e`) gives every mark room to breathe, while the single Adwaita blue (`--accent` `#3584e4`) establishes identity through calm technological confidence. No gradients, no glows, no decorative geometry — the site's own tokens carry the whole composition.

The mark is the message. The brand symbol sits centered horizontally and optically centered vertically, with the wordmark and one concise idea stacked beneath it as a single measured group. Alignment and spacing should feel meticulously calibrated, with the restraint expected from a master-level identity system.

Color is sparse and purposeful: luminous blue, cool white (`--ink` `#f4f4f4`), and quiet gray (`--ink-dim` `#c7c7c7`). Contrast creates hierarchy without noise.

Typography is an anchor, not an explanation. The name in Bricolage Grotesque Bold and one line in Instrument Sans are enough; scale, spacing, and position do the remaining work. The result should feel confidently legible at thumbnail size and meticulously crafted at full resolution.

The final composition is a centered mass surrounded by generous negative space. Nothing competes with the identity, and no detail exists merely to fill the canvas: exact, calm, and unmistakably intentional.
